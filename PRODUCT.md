# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro with React integration for Lucide React icons.

## Users

Athletes and people with performance, rehabilitation, or clinically informed training needs who are evaluating Carlos Ramírez as a coach.

## Product Purpose

Present Carlos Ramírez's qualifications, services, athlete outcomes, and contact path so prospective clients can assess fit and start a WhatsApp conversation.

## Positioning

High-performance physical preparation combined with sports-medicine awareness, post-injury progression, and programs adapted to clinical history.

## Operating Context

Visitors compare credentials and services on mobile or desktop, then contact Carlos through WhatsApp for either in-person or online training.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Single-page Spanish marketing site in Astro.
- Preserve the live Framer site's exact blue, black, and white palette.
- Use Lucide React icons for interface iconography.
- Images and social destinations remain clearly marked placeholders until the client supplies final assets and URLs.
- No backend, CMS, analytics, or dynamic data integration in this implementation.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Carlos Ramírez.
- Preserve the factual Spanish copy and the existing athletic, high-performance positioning.
- Follow the component, typography, spacing, border, and shadow guidance in `design-system.pen`; the live Framer palette overrides its existing accent palette.

## Evidence on Hand

- Live reference: `https://joyful-wealth-802705.framer.app`.
- Supplied credentials, awards, services, and named athlete proof from the live page.
- No approved final imagery or social URLs; placeholders must not be mistaken for production assets.

## Product Principles

- Lead with credible expertise, not hype.
- Make the service fit legible for athletes and clinical-history clients.
- Keep the contact path direct and visible.
- Treat missing client assets as explicit, easy-to-replace placeholders.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic structure, keyboard-visible focus, sufficient contrast, reduced-motion support, and responsive layouts across mobile, tablet, and desktop.
